2. Present three short lists: late (days late, worst first), waiting for approval (how long the draft has sat), due soon (by date).
3. For anything late, offer `/draft-chasers`. For a draft waiting, offer to review it with the operator and then `approve P-xxxx`.
4. A draft flagged as a testimonial for therapeutic goods in Australia cannot be approved: say so and point to `/compliance`.
